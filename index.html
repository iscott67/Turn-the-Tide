About Turn the Tide

The right drink for the moment.

Turn the Tide is a personal AI bartender for the home enthusiast. It helps you understand what your bar can make, choose a cocktail for the occasion and gradually learn your taste.

Rather than functioning as a conventional recipe search engine, it combines inventory, context and preference to make a small number of useful recommendations.

Privacy

Your information

Inventory, favourites, preferences and cocktail history are stored locally in this browser. They are not synchronized to another device and may be lost if browser data is cleared.

Photographs selected for bottle recognition are sent to the configured AI service through the app's Netlify Function for analysis. Do not include people, private documents or unrelated personal information in bar photographs.

This alpha build does not yet include user accounts, cloud backup or cross-device synchronization.

Responsible use

Enjoy responsibly

Turn the Tide is intended only for adults of legal drinking age. Recommendations are for recipe and entertainment purposes and are not a measure of safe alcohol consumption.

Never drink and drive. Do not serve alcohol to minors or anyone who should not consume it for medical, legal or personal reasons.

Help

Quick guide

Build your bar

Use Scan for overlapping shelf photos, then correct every detected item before saving.

Choose a drink

Open Tonight, select mood, occasion and strength, then review the top three suggestions.

Teach your taste

Rate drinks after making them. Detailed feedback improves later recommendations.

Install the app

Use Install App in the menu when supported. On iPhone, use Safari's Add to Home Screen command.

Administrative

Reset app data

This permanently removes inventory, wishlist, ratings, favourites, taste learning and saved recommendation settings from this device.
