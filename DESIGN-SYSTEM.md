# Turn the Tide Design System 2.0

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Principles
**Calm:** remove visual noise and preserve one obvious primary action.
**Confident:** use direct labels, clear hierarchy and restrained emphasis.
**Warm:** feel knowledgeable and welcoming rather than clinical.

## Typography
Interface text uses the native system sans-serif stack. Cocktail names may use Iowan Old Style, Palatino or Georgia. Serif type is never used for ingredients, instructions, forms or navigation.

Weights:
- 400 body
- 500 buttons and labels
- 600 section headings
- 700 key numbers only

## Spacing
4, 8, 16, 24, 32 and 48 px.

## Components
Cards use a 22 px radius, subtle border and restrained shadow. Primary buttons are filled. Secondary buttons use a tinted surface. Tertiary actions are text-only.

## Themes
Atlantic, Modern, Cellar and Midnight all use semantic tokens rather than component-specific hard-coded colours.

## Navigation
Primary: Home, Scan, My Bar, Taste and Bar IQ.
Secondary and administrative actions belong in the hamburger menu.

## Motion
140 ms press feedback and 220 ms transitions. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

## Accessibility
44 px touch targets, visible focus, 4.5:1 contrast, screen-heading focus after navigation, increased-contrast support and no colour-only status cues.

## Measurement presentation

Recipe quantities always retain both metric and imperial values. The user's preferred system appears first in the stronger type treatment; the alternate system appears immediately below in secondary text. The preference is controlled under Appearance and persists locally.
