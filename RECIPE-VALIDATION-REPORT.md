## Important editorial distinction

Classic and IBA-linked records are marked separately from Turn the Tide house recipes. Atlantic-themed recipes and other original variations are not presented as historical classics.

Automated schema validation confirms completeness and internal consistency. It does **not** replace physical sensory testing. Every house recipe is therefore labelled “sensory validation pending” until it has been mixed and approved by a human tester.
