# Phase 10 — Delight and Product Polish

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Objective
Add restrained moments of polish that make the application feel responsive and considered without becoming distracting.

## Included
- First-run onboarding
- Subtle feedback setting
- Celebration-moment setting
- Browser vibration where supported
- Milestone cards on Home
- Restrained visual celebration after meaningful actions
- More consistent press, hover and dialog motion
- Reduced-motion support
- Experience settings in the hamburger menu
- Reset support for onboarding and experience preferences

## Platform reality
Vibration support varies by browser. It is generally available on many Android browsers and is not reliably available in iPhone Safari. Visual feedback remains the fallback.
