# Phase 9 — Personalization

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Objective
Make recommendations improve over time without requiring a complicated profile setup.

## Included
- My Taste profile dashboard
- Six taste dimensions
- Manual More, Neutral and Less controls
- Learned taste patterns from ratings, favourites and history
- House Favourite collection
- Cocktail journal
- Notes and rating history
- Discovery-level setting
- Default-strength preference
- Recommendation scoring that reflects personalization
- Clear History while preserving favourites and manual preferences

## Privacy
Personalization data remains in local browser storage. This release does not create an account or synchronize data between devices.
