# Phase 6 — Smart Shopping

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Objective
Turn the shopping list into a ranked decision tool rather than a plain checklist.

## Included
- Best-value bottle rankings
- Potential cocktail-unlock impact
- Running-low and replacement section
- Optional ideas section
- Already Have action
- Shopping List action
- Ignore action
- Saved Bottle List
- Completion checkboxes
- Clear-completed function
- Shopping impact summary

## Constraint
Suggestions are based on the curated recipe library and current inventory. Price is not included because the app does not yet hold reliable local pricing data.
