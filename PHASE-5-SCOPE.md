# Phase 5 — Bar Compass

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Objective
Turn inventory analysis into an interactive map of the user's collection and cocktail capability.

## Included
- Interactive radial Bar Compass
- Spirit-family segments
- Bottle counts by category
- Ready-now and one-ingredient-away recipe counts
- Tap-to-explore category detail
- Highest-value missing ingredient by category
- Direct shopping-list action
- Ready-cocktail shortcuts
- Updated cocktail landscape and programme coverage language

## Interpretation
The Compass reflects the inventory types and recipe requirements recorded in the app. It is not a valuation of bottle quality or price.
