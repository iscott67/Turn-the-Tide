# Phase 8 — My Bar

## Version
3.3.10-dev

## Objective
Turn inventory from a functional list into an organized, informative bottle collection.

## Included
- My Bar summary and health metrics
- Spirit-family filtering
- Search, status filtering and sorting
- Bottle-card layout
- Bottle status badges
- Per-bottle ready and total recipe counts
- Bottle detail sheet
- Recipe shortcuts from bottle details
- Edit Bottle and Change Status actions
- Notes display
- Empty-state scan guidance
- Keyboard-accessible bottle cards

## Design choice
The bottle illustrations are generated with interface styling rather than external product photography. This keeps the build lightweight and visually consistent while product-image support remains a future enhancement.
